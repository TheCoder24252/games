<p align='center'>TheCoder24252's Maze Game is a fun, endless puzzle game for both web browsers and desktops. Every time you play, the game automatically builds a completely unique maze from scratch, so you will never get the same map twice! You can play solo to race the clock, or jump into a 2-player mode to challenge your friends. Clear levels to earn shop credits, unlock awesome custom player trails, and save your high scores to your account. You can even test your skills with crazy variations like the "Fog of War" darkness and if you earn enough credits you can get your account on the leaderboard.
